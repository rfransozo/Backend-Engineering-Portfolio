Design Philosophy

How I approach engineering

These repositories are deliberate simulations of real engineering problems — not tutorials. They reflect how I think and build in practice.

Clear ownership

Every component has a defined responsibility. Ambiguity is resolved in design, not at runtime.

Explicit trade-offs

Non-goals are documented, not hidden. The reasoning behind decisions matters as much as the code.

Simplicity first

No premature complexity. Systems grow in response to real constraints, not anticipated ones.

Operability

Systems that can realistically run in production — observable, deployable, and maintainable.
